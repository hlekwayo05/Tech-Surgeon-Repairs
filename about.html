Who We Are

Founded in 2020, Tech Surgeon started as a small repair shop with a big vision: to provide reliable, affordable, and fast tech repair services to the people of South Africa. What began as a passion project has grown into one of the most trusted names in device repairs across the country.

Our team of certified technicians brings together years of experience and a genuine love for technology. We understand how important your devices are to your daily life, which is why we treat every repair with the care and attention it deserves.

At Tech Surgeon, we believe in transparency, quality service, and building lasting relationships with our customers. Whether you're dealing with a cracked screen, a faulty battery, or any other tech issue, we're here to help you stay connected to what matters most.
