Frequently Asked Questions

How long do repairs usually take?

Most repairs are completed within 1-2 business days. Screen replacements and other common repairs can often be done while you wait (approximately 1-2 hours). More complex repairs may take longer, and we'll provide you with an estimated completion time after diagnosing the issue.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes, all our repairs come with a 12-month warranty on both parts and labor. This means if the same issue occurs again or if there's a problem with the parts we installed, we'll fix it at no additional cost to you.

Do I need to back up my data before bringing in my device?

While we take every precaution to protect your data, we strongly recommend backing up your device before any repair. For certain repairs, especially those involving the logic board or storage components, there's always a small risk of data loss. If you need assistance with data backup, please let us know in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, and mobile payment options like SnapScan and Zapper. Payment is due upon completion of the repair.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes! We offer a 10% student discount with a valid student ID. We also run seasonal promotions and offer discounts for multiple device repairs. Follow us on social media to stay updated on our latest deals.
